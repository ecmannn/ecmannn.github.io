This page exists for one reason: Google asks every application that connects to a Google account to publish a privacy policy, and I keep a small personal tool, registered with Google as "research-desk", that reads my own Google Calendar.

The tool is used by one person, me. It runs on my own computer and inside my own note-taking software. With my permission it reads events from my calendar so that they can be shown next to my notes and deadlines. It does not write to the calendar, does not read anyone else's data, and does not send, share, or sell anything to anyone. The access token Google issues is stored only on my computer, and I can revoke it at any time from the permissions page of my Google account.

If you have reached this page from a Google consent screen and you are not me, you should not grant the application access to your account.
